Sources

  • Sherwin-Williams paint calculator FAQ: a gallon typically covers 350–400 sq ft; a quart about 100; primer about 200–300. sherwin-williams.com
  • Quikrete Concrete Mix 1101 data sheet (Oct 2022): 60 lb ≈ 0.45 cu ft, 80 lb ≈ 0.60 cu ft. PDF
  • Mulch volume: cu ft = sq ft × inches ÷ 12; 1 cu yd = 27 cu ft, so 1 yard covers 108 sq ft at 3 inches. Home Depot’s public mulch calculator labels results as estimates only.
  • Tile layout waste: consumer-safe midpoints of 10% straight, 15% offset, 20% diagonal/herringbone, consistent with contractor estimating ranges (not a substitute for TCNA project specs).
  • Drywall sheet area is geometry: 4×8 = 32 sq ft. Waste default 15%. CertainTeed’s public calculator does not subtract openings and does not include waste.
  • Wallpaper: US double-roll listed area is commonly ~56 sq ft; this tool defaults to 80% usable after match and waste.
  • Moving boxes: midpoints of published per-room ranges (kitchen / bedroom / living mixes).
  • Room cost: 2025–2026 published remodel bands of about $15–$30 / sq ft cosmetic and much higher for wet rooms (Angi / HomeAdvisor summaries). The kitchen multiplier (2.5×) is ours and is labeled as planning only.
  • Bathroom cost bands (recalibrated October 2026): a fixed base plus a per-sq-ft part. Cosmetic $2,000–$4,000 + $25–$100 / sq ft, mid $5,000–$10,000 + $100–$200, full $6,000–$14,000 + $250–$400. A 5×8 (40 sq ft) bath gives $3,000–$8,000 / $9,000–$18,000 / $16,000–$30,000. Sources: This Old House small-bathroom remodel cost guide (updated June 8, 2026, Homewyse data, value-grade materials, medium-cost labor): 5×8 at $15,900–$20,100, a full-replacement scope that matches our full tier; its scope tiers are basic refresh $3,000–$8,000 and mid-range update $8,000–$18,000 (thisoldhouse.com); JLC/Zonda 2025 Cost vs. Value, Bath Remodel | Midrange: $26,138 national for a 5×7 bath with every fixture replaced, also our full tier (jlconline.com); Fixr bathroom remodel cost (updated January 27, 2026): 5×8 $2,800–$16,000, partial $6,000–$25,000, full gut $9,000–$30,000+ (fixr.com); Angi bathroom remodel cost guide (updated July 9, 2026): $70–$250 per sq ft, minor $3,000–$10,000, partial $10,000–$25,000, full $25,000–$80,000 (angi.com); HomeAdvisor True Cost Guide (updated June 17, 2026): typical $6,651–$17,660, half bath $1,500–$15,000, full bath $3,500–$20,000, primary $7,000–$30,000 (homeadvisor.com).
