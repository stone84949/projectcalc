Hotshot rates per mile in 2026

Hotshot rate ranges are mostly published as guides - by news sites, load boards and companies that sell factoring or tracking - each with its own range. Here they are as published:

  • FreightWaves Checkpoint, May 18, 2026Most loads $1.50-$2.00/mi; urgent or complex $3+/mi; easy runs in competitive markets closer to $1/mi
  • Porter Freight Funding (a factoring company), last updated Jul 1, 2026Average around $1.50/mi, range $1.00-$3.00+; standard loads $1.25-$1.75; expedited, oversize or specialized $2.50-$4.00+
  • Matrack (a GPS tracking company), Mar 13, 2026Typically $1.50-$3.00/mi
  • Truckstop (a load board), Dec 23, 2024Generally $1.50/mi; urgent loads up to $2/mi; low end $1.00-$1.25/mi
  • DAT Flatbed Report, Oct 19, 2026 - Class 8 flatbed, not hotshotNational flatbed spot linehaul $2.65/mi, fuel excluded

The ranges overlap but don't agree. They were written at different times, with diesel at very different prices, for different freight, and most come from companies that sell to carriers. Treat them as a rough map, then check live lane rates on a load board before quoting.

Average hotshot rate per mile

Where the sources overlap, ordinary hotshot loads sit around $1.50-$2.00 per loaded mile, with $1.50 the single figure quoted most often (Porter, Truckstop) and $2.50-$4.00+ reserved for urgent, oversize or specialized freight. That's a published range, not a measured average: none of the hotshot guides says how many loads its figure is based on.

DAT's $2.65 is a different market - Class 8 flatbeds, paid linehaul with fuel surcharges excluded - so it isn't a hotshot benchmark. Fuel surcharges come on top of it, so check whether a rate you're comparing includes fuel before lining the two up.

Why diesel changes the answer this year

The U.S. average on-highway diesel price was $6.276 a gallon in the week of October 19, 2026, up $2.656 from a year earlier (EIA). Guides written before the run-up, like Truckstop's and Matrack's, priced their ranges against much cheaper fuel. At 10 mpg, $6.28 diesel is about 63 cents of every mile driven, loaded or empty, before anything else gets paid.

The examples below use $6.28 a gallon. Diesel varies a lot by region - put your own price into the load panel.

How much do hotshot drivers make per load?

Gross per load is the rate times the loaded miles: at $1.50-$2.00 a mile, a 500-mile load grosses $750-$1,000. What's left after fuel and factoring is a lot smaller. Three example loads, run through the same math as the load panel:

"Net to truck" still has to pay the insurance, truck payment, maintenance and the driver. That's what the minimum is for: the first load nets less than the truck costs to run, even though $1.75 sits inside the published average.

How much do hotshot drivers make per mile?

Per mile, take-home is the rate minus the cost per mile - and the cost per mile is yours, not an average. The cost per mile calculator's example operator ($3,200 a month in fixed costs over 8,000 miles, $0.15/mi maintenance, $0.50/mi driver pay, 20% deadhead, $0.25/mi profit) at $6.28 diesel:

Fixed - $3,200 / 8,000 mi$0.400

Fuel - $6.28 / 10 mpg$0.628

Maintenance and tires$0.150

Driver pay$0.500

All-in cost per mile$1.678

Minimum rate per loaded mile, with profit and deadhead$2.41

The same operator at $3.65 diesel - close to the $3.62 EIA average a year earlier - needs $2.09. At $6.28 the floor is $2.41 per loaded mile - above the $1.50-$2.00 range most guides call average. Your number will differ; work it out before quoting.

How much do hotshot drivers make a week and a year?

Same example operator, paying for the truck's fixed costs, fuel and maintenance but not the driver - so what's left is the owner's pay, before factoring, tolls and income tax. EXAMPLE: 2,000 miles a week, 1,600 of them loaded, at $1.178 a mile in costs ($2,356 a week):

Published pay figures land all over that range because they measure different things. FreightWaves (May 18, 2026) quotes salary sites for hotshot drivers as of December 2024: ZipRecruiter $58,947 a year, Glassdoor $45,841, Salary.com $41,540 - pay figures, not an owner-operator's profit. The same article cites unnamed industry estimates of $60,000-$120,000 a year in gross income for top owner-operators, which is revenue before the costs above. Neither is what an owner-operator takes home; the table is how to work out your own.

Common questions

What is the average hotshot rate per mile in 2026?

The 2026 guides above put ordinary loads around $1.50-$2.00 per loaded mile, with urgent, oversize or specialized freight at $2.50-$4.00 or more. They're published ranges, not a measured market average, and they don't all agree.

Is $2 a mile good for hotshot?

Only if it clears your cost per mile. The example operator above needs $2.41 per loaded mile at $6.28 diesel, so $2.00 loses money for that truck. Work out your own floor with the cost per mile calculator.

Why don't hotshot rate sources agree?

Different dates, different fuel prices, different freight, and no shared data set. Several are written by companies that sell factoring, dispatch or tracking to carriers. Use them for a ballpark and your own lanes for the real number.

How much do hotshot drivers make a year?

Salary sites put hotshot driver pay around $41,500-$59,000 a year (as of December 2024, via FreightWaves). Owner-operators keep what's left after the truck's costs, which the table above shows can be almost nothing at $1.50 a mile or about $79,000 at $2.50 for the same example truck.

Do I need a CDL to haul hotshot?

It depends on your truck and trailer ratings. The hotshot weight calculator checks them against the federal 26,001 lb line.
