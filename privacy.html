Privacy

There is no account and no server-side form. Calculator inputs are saved in your browser’s localStorage so a refresh does not wipe the room. Clear site data to delete them.

If a PayPal payment link is added later, PayPal’s privacy policy applies to that checkout. This static site does not see your payment details.

We do not run an ad network on this first release. If display ads are added later, this page will name the vendor.

ProjectCalc is a planning tool. Numbers are estimates. Confirm coverage on the product you actually buy.
