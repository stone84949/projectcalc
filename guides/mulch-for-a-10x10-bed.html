How much mulch for a 10×10 bed?

A 10×10 bed 3 inches deep takes 13 bags of 2 cu ft mulch, or 1 cubic yard in bulk. Table for 2, 3 and 4 inches.

A 10×10 bed 3 inches deep takes 13 bags of 2 cu ft mulch, or 1 cubic yard in bulk.

10 × 10 is 100 sq ft. 100 × 3 ÷ 12 = 25 cu ft. Divide by the 2 cu ft on the bag to get 12.5, and round up to 13. In bulk, 25 ÷ 27 = 0.93 cubic yards, so you order 1.

With 1.5 cu ft bags the 3-inch bed is 17 bags. The calculator rounds bags and bulk yards up, because a partial bag is still a full bag at the register. At 4 inches the bed passes one yard, which doubles the bulk order. At 3 inches it stays just under.

Most beds want 2–3 inches. Keep mulch pulled back from stems and trunks. A bed that is not a rectangle can be split into rectangles, with the totals added.

FAQ

How many square feet does one bag cover?

A 2 cu ft bag covers 8 sq ft at 3 inches deep. The 10×10 bed is 100 sq ft, so 12.5 bags, and you buy 13.

Bulk or bags for a 10×10 bed?

At 3 inches the bed takes 0.93 cubic yards. Compare the bulk price for one yard with 13 bags of the size you like. The calculator takes a bulk price per yard if you want to compare.

How deep should the mulch be?

Most beds want 2–3 inches. Deeper than 4 inches can smother roots.
