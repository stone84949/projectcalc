How many bags of concrete for a 4×4 slab?

A 4×4 slab 4 inches thick takes 10 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste, or 13 bags of 60 lb. 6 inches takes 14 bags of 80 lb.

A 4×4 slab 4 inches thick takes 10 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste, or 13 bags of 60 lb. At 6 inches thick it takes 14 bags of 80 lb.

4 × 4 × (4 ÷ 12) = 5.33 cu ft. Five percent waste brings it to 5.6 cu ft. Quikrete’s data sheet for Concrete Mix 1101 lists about 0.60 cu ft per 80 lb bag, so 5.6 ÷ 0.60 = 9.3 bags, rounded up to 10. At 0.45 cu ft for a 60 lb bag it is 12.4, which is 13.

This is slab volume only: length × width × thickness. A post hole or a round footing is a cylinder, not a slab, so this calculator does not size it. Gravel base, forms and any reinforcement are also separate. Check local code and the product data before you pour.

FAQ

How many 60 lb bags for a 4×4 slab?

13 bags with 5% waste at 4 inches thick, and 19 at 6 inches. The 60 lb bag yields about 0.45 cu ft.

Does the count include waste?

Yes, 5%. With 0% waste a 4-inch 4×4 slab is 9 bags of 80 lb, and a 6-inch slab is 14.

Can I use this for a fence post?

No. A post hole is a cylinder, and this calculator sizes rectangular slabs. Follow the post maker’s or a fence installer’s guidance for post footings.
