How many boxes of flooring for 200, 500 or 1,000 sq ft?

500 sq ft of flooring laid straight takes 550 sq ft with 10% waste: 28 boxes at 20 sq ft per box. Table for 200 to 1,000 sq ft.

The first two columns assume 20 sq ft per box. Laminate, LVP and engineered hardwood boxes often hold 18 to 24 sq ft, so the last column shows the straight layout with 24 sq ft boxes. The calculator loads 500 sq ft as a 20×25 floor; for a different area, enter any length and width that multiply to it.

The waste is for end cuts and offcuts you cannot reuse: 10% straight, 15% diagonal and 20% herringbone. A whole-house job with many rooms and doorways leans toward the high end, so add closets and hallways as their own areas in the square footage calculator first.

FAQ

Is 10% waste enough for flooring?

For a straight lay in simple rectangular rooms, usually. Diagonal is 15% and herringbone 20%. Lots of small rooms and doorways use more.

Should I buy an extra box?

Yes, from the same lot. A matching box for a repair later is hard to find once the lot or the style is gone.

How do I enter 500 sq ft if my room is not 20×25?

Enter your real length and width, or any pair that multiplies to the total, like 10 × 50. Only the area and the layout change the answer.
