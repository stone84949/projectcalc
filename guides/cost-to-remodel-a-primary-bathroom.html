2026 cost to remodel a primary bathroom

A 10×10 primary bathroom plans at $15,000–$30,000 for a mid-range update and $31,000–$54,000 for a full remodel, next to 2026 published ranges.

A 10×10 primary bathroom plans at $15,000–$30,000 for a mid-range update that keeps the layout. Replacing every fixture, the tub or shower, and the tile plans at $31,000–$54,000.

Bathrooms use their own bands: a fixed base for fixtures, plumbing, waterproofing and permits, plus a per-sq-ft part. For a mid update that is $5,000–$10,000 plus $100–$200 per sq ft. A 10×10 bath is 100 sq ft, so $5,000 + $10,000 = $15,000 low and $10,000 + $20,000 = $30,000 high. Because the base is fixed, a bigger bathroom costs less per square foot than a small one.

FAQ

Why is a primary bathroom cheaper per square foot than a small one?

The fixed base covers fixtures, plumbing, waterproofing and permits whatever the size. A 5×8 mid update is $9,000–$18,000 for 40 sq ft; a 10×10 is $15,000–$30,000 for 100 sq ft.

What pushes a primary bath past the full tier?

Moving the toilet, shower or drains, a curbless or steam shower, custom tile and luxury fixtures. Get bids for those.

Which finish should I pick for a double vanity and a new shower?

If the layout stays and you replace the vanity, toilet, flooring and some tile, pick mid. If the tub or shower, all the tile and every fixture go, pick full.
