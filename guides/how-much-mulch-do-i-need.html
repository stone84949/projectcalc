How much mulch do I need?

Cubic yards and bags from bed size. One yard covers 108 square feet at 3 inches.

Mulch is sold by the cubic yard or the 2 cu ft bag. The geometry is the same either way: area × depth.

A 10×20 bed is 200 sq ft. Three inches is 200 × 3 ÷ 12 = 50 cubic feet. Divide by 27 and you have 1.85 cubic yards, so you order two. In bags: 50 ÷ 2 = 25 even.

Most landscape beds want 2–3 inches. Deeper than 4 inches smothers roots. The Home Depot public calculator also treats these figures as estimates only.
