Tile calculator with waste

Why a straight tile layout needs about 10% extra and herringbone needs about 20%.

Tile waste is not a fudge factor for bad measuring. It is the offcuts you cannot put back on the floor. A straight grid leaves rectangular leftovers you can often spin into the next run. A diagonal or herringbone layout hits every wall at an angle, so the leftover is a triangle.

This calculator uses 10% for straight, 15% for offset, and 20% for diagonal or herringbone. Those are consumer-safe midpoints of ranges cited by tile contractors and layout guides, not a TCNA code requirement for your specific room.

Buy a spare box from the same lot. Matching a discontinued dye lot later costs more than the extra box today.
