How much paint for a bedroom?

Most bedrooms take 2 gallons for the walls at two coats. A 12×14 bedroom takes 3 gallons once you add the ceiling.

Most bedrooms need 2 gallons for two coats on the walls. A 12×14 bedroom with 8 ft ceilings, one door and two windows needs 3 gallons if you paint the ceiling too (1,066 sq ft of paint work).

Every row below uses 8 ft ceilings, one door, two windows, two coats and 375 sq ft per gallon. Those are the calculator defaults. Change any of them in the calculator.

BedroomWalls onlyWalls + ceiling
10×102 gallons (538 sq ft)2 gallons (738 sq ft)
10×122 gallons (602 sq ft)3 gallons (842 sq ft)
12×122 gallons (666 sq ft)3 gallons (954 sq ft)
12×142 gallons (730 sq ft)3 gallons (1,066 sq ft)
14×163 gallons (858 sq ft)4 gallons (1,306 sq ft)

The square footage in each row is the area times the number of coats. For the 12×14 room: walls are 2 × (12 + 14) × 8 = 416 sq ft, minus 51 sq ft of door and windows is 365, plus the 168 sq ft ceiling is 533, and two coats is 1,066. Divided by 375 that is 2.84 gallons, so you buy 3.

Many people paint the ceiling with a separate ceiling paint. If you do, run the walls and the ceiling as two separate calculations.
