How much flooring for a 15×15 room?

13 boxes of flooring at 20 sq ft per box for a 15×15 room laid straight, or 14 boxes herringbone. Table from 10×10 to 1,000 sq ft.

Buy 13 boxes at 20 sq ft per box for a 15×15 room laid straight. A herringbone layout needs 14 boxes.

15 × 15 is 225 sq ft. A straight layout adds 10% waste: 247.5 sq ft. 247.5 ÷ 20 = 12.4 boxes, so 13. Diagonal is 15% waste, 258.75 sq ft, still 13 boxes. Herringbone is 20%, 270 sq ft, which is 14.

The first four rows are a single room. The last two are whole-floor totals, entered as a length and width that multiply to the area. The straight and diagonal columns assume 20 sq ft per box, and the last column shows 24 sq ft boxes. Laminate, LVP and engineered hardwood boxes often hold 18 to 24 sq ft, so check the number printed on yours. For the 15×15 room laid straight, 18 sq ft boxes means 14 boxes and 24 sq ft boxes means 11.

Closets and doorways count. Add them as extra area in the square footage calculator first, and keep a spare box from the same lot.

FAQ

How many square feet is a 15×15 room?

225 sq ft. With 10% waste for a straight layout you buy 247.5 sq ft of flooring.

Is 10% waste enough for a square room?

For a straight lay, usually. Diagonal is 15% and herringbone 20%. A room with closets, jogs or lots of doorways leans higher.

What if my room is not a perfect square?

Split it into rectangles, add their areas, and enter any length and width that multiply to the total. Only the area and the layout change the answer.
