How many moving boxes do I need? Studio to 3-bedroom

About 34 boxes for a studio, 54 for a 1-bedroom, 64 for a 2-bedroom and 77 for a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home.

A typical 2-bedroom takes about 64 boxes. A studio takes about 34, a 1-bedroom 54, and a 3-bedroom, 2-bath home 77, or 98 with a dining room and a garage.

Each room adds a fixed mix of box sizes, taken from the midpoints of published per-room ranges. The calculator adds the rooms, then rounds each size up. How much stuff you have matters as much as the room count: the same 2-bedroom is about 49 boxes if it is sparse (0.75×) and about 87 if it is packed (1.35×).

Books and canned goods go in small boxes so they stay liftable. Linens and pillows go in large ones. Dish packs have thicker walls for the kitchen.
