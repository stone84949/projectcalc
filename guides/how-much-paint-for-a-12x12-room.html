How much paint for a 12×12 room?

Worked paint math for a 12×12 bedroom, including doors, windows, and two coats.

People search this because the can says “350–400 square feet” and a 12×12 room is 144 square feet, so one gallon “should” be enough. That is floor area. Walls are not the floor.

At 8 ft ceilings the four walls are 2 × (12 + 12) × 8 = 384 sq ft. Subtract one 3×7 door (21) and two 3×5 windows (30) and you have 333 sq ft of wall. Two coats is 666 sq ft. Mid-range coverage of 375 sq ft per gallon is 1.776 gallons. You buy two.

If you also paint the ceiling, add 144 sq ft per coat. Primer covers less — Sherwin-Williams publishes about 200–300 sq ft per gallon — so do not use the topcoat number for primer.
