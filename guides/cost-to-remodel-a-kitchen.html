2026 cost to remodel a kitchen

ProjectCalc planning bands for a 10×10 to 15×20 kitchen at cosmetic, mid and full finish. A 10×12 mid-range kitchen plans at $12,000–$24,000.

A 10×12 kitchen plans at $12,000–$24,000 for a mid remodel on ProjectCalc’s bands: new surfaces plus some trades. A cosmetic refresh of the same room plans at $4,500–$9,000 and a full gut at $30,000–$60,000.

The math is floor area × a per-square-foot band × a kitchen factor. 10 × 12 is 120 sq ft. The mid band is $40–$80 per sq ft, which is $4,800–$9,600 for a plain room. Kitchens carry plumbing, cabinets, counters and appliance circuits, so the calculator applies a 2.5× kitchen factor: $12,000–$24,000.

The cosmetic band ($15–$30 per sq ft) follows 2025–2026 published remodel summaries from Angi and HomeAdvisor. The mid and full bands and the 2.5× kitchen factor are ProjectCalc’s own planning numbers, listed on the sources page. Neither is a quote. Cabinet grade and whether the sink, range or walls move change the number more than the room size does.

Use this as a starting budget envelope. Then get at least two local bids that list the cabinets, counters and trades they include.

FAQ

Is this what a contractor will charge me?

No. These are national planning bands. Local labor, permits, cabinet choice and what is behind the walls move the real number.

Why does a kitchen cost more than a bedroom of the same size?

Plumbing, cabinets, counters, appliance circuits and ventilation. The calculator models that with a 2.5× factor on the general bands. The factor is our planning number, not a published figure.

Does a bigger kitchen always cost more per job?

On these bands, yes, because the cost scales with floor area. Real kitchens do not scale that cleanly: a long wall of cabinets costs more than open floor.
