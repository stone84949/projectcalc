2026 cost to tile a bathroom

Planning range for tiling a bathroom, using floor area, waste, and 2026 cited bathroom cost bands.

There is no honest single number. A 5×8 bath floor is 40 sq ft. With 10% waste you buy 44 sq ft of tile. At $4–$12 per sq ft the material is $176–$528 before thinset, grout, membrane, and a wet-saw blade.

Labor dominates. Industry 2025–2026 remodel writeups still put light cosmetic work near $15–$30 per sq ft and wet-room work much higher. For whole-bathroom work this site uses bathroom bands set from 2026 cost guides: a 5×8 bath plans at $3,000–$8,000 for a cosmetic refresh, $9,000–$18,000 for a mid-range update and $16,000–$30,000 for a full remodel. This Old House (June 2026, Homewyse data, value-grade materials) puts a full 5×8 remodel at $15,900–$20,100, which sits in our full tier. It is a planning range, not what a Knoxville tile setter will quote after they see the subfloor.
