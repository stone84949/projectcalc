2026 cost to tile a bathroom

Planning range for tiling a bathroom, using floor area, waste, and remodel intensity.

There is no honest single number. A 5×8 bath floor is 40 sq ft. With 10% waste you buy 44 sq ft of tile. At $4–$12 per sq ft the material is $176–$528 before thinset, grout, membrane, and a wet-saw blade.

Labor dominates. Industry 2025–2026 remodel writeups still put light cosmetic work near $15–$30 per sq ft and wet-room work much higher. This site’s bathroom intensity (2.2×) on the mid band is a planning range, not what a Knoxville tile setter will quote after they see the subfloor.
