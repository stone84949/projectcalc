2026 cost to remodel a small bathroom

Published 2026 estimates for a 5×8 bathroom remodel, next to ProjectCalc’s planning bands and the math behind them.

This Old House puts a 5×8 bathroom remodel at $15,900–$20,100 in its small-bathroom cost guide, updated June 8, 2026. It bases that on Homewyse costs for value-grade materials and medium-cost labor. The same guide lists $3,000–$8,000 for a basic refresh and $8,000–$18,000 for a mid-range update. (This Old House)

ProjectCalc’s planning bands

The room cost calculator multiplies floor area by a per-square-foot band, then by 2.2 for a bathroom, because water, tile and ventilation cost more than a bedroom. The bands are our own planning numbers, not quotes.

Finish bandMath (40 sq ft × band × 2.2)5×8 range
Cosmetic ($15–$30 / sq ft)40 × $15–$30 × 2.2$1,320–$2,640
Mid remodel ($40–$80 / sq ft)40 × $40–$80 × 2.2$3,520–$7,040
Full gut ($100–$200 / sq ft)40 × $100–$200 × 2.2$8,800–$17,600

Read them side by side. Our “mid” band lands inside what This Old House calls a basic refresh. Our “full gut” band ends below its full 5×8 estimate. Treat the calculator as a low starting budget, and expect a real bid to come in higher if the job moves plumbing or replaces the tub or shower.

Other small sizes on the same bands: a 5×7 bath (35 sq ft) is $3,080–$6,160 mid and $7,700–$15,400 full gut. A 6×8 bath (48 sq ft) is $4,224–$8,448 mid and $10,560–$21,120 full gut.
