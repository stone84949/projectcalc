2026 cost to remodel a small bathroom

Published 2026 estimates for a 5×8 bathroom remodel, next to ProjectCalc’s planning bands and the math behind them.

This Old House puts a 5×8 bathroom remodel at $15,900–$20,100 in its small-bathroom cost guide, updated June 8, 2026. It bases that on Homewyse costs for value-grade materials and medium-cost labor. The same guide lists $3,000–$8,000 for a basic refresh and $8,000–$18,000 for a mid-range update. (This Old House)

ProjectCalc’s planning bands

Bathrooms get their own 2026 cost bands instead of the room multiplier: a fixed base for fixtures, plumbing, waterproofing and permits, plus a per-square-foot part. Small baths cost more per square foot and large primary baths less. The bands are our own planning numbers, not quotes.

Read them side by side. Our “mid” band lands inside what This Old House calls a mid-range update ($8,000–$18,000), and our “full gut” band brackets its full 5×8 estimate of $15,900–$20,100. Treat the calculator as a starting budget, and expect a real bid to come in higher if the job moves plumbing or replaces the tub or shower.

Other small sizes on the same bands: a 5×7 bath (35 sq ft) is $8,500–$17,000 mid and $14,750–$28,000 full gut. A 6×8 bath (48 sq ft) is $9,800–$19,600 mid and $18,000–$33,200 full gut.
