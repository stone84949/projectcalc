How many rolls of wallpaper for a 12×12 room?

8 double rolls for a 12×12 room with 8 ft walls, one door and two windows, at 44.8 usable sq ft per roll.

Buy 8 double rolls for a 12×12 room with 8 ft walls, one door and two windows.

The walls are 384 sq ft. Take off 51 sq ft of door and windows and 333 sq ft is left. A US double roll commonly lists about 56 sq ft. After trimming and pattern matching, the calculator counts 80% of that as usable: 44.8 sq ft. 333 ÷ 44.8 = 7.43, so you buy 8.
