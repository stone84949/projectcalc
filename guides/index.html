DIY materials guides

Each guide answers one question in the first line, shows the math, and runs the live calculator with the same numbers. Estimates only.

Paint calculator

  • How much paint for a 12×12 room? Worked paint math for a 12×12 bedroom, including doors, windows, and two coats.
  • How much paint for a 10×10 room? Two gallons for two coats on the walls of a 10×10 room with 8 ft ceilings. One gallon for a single coat.
  • How much paint for a bedroom? Most bedrooms take 2 gallons for the walls at two coats. A 12×14 bedroom takes 3 gallons once you add the ceiling.
  • How much paint for a living room? 3 gallons for two coats on the walls of a 15×20 living room with 8 ft ceilings, two doorways and three windows. 5 gallons with the ceiling.
  • How much paint for a small bathroom? 1 gallon covers two coats on the walls of a 5×8 bathroom with 8 ft ceilings, one door and one window. Add the ceiling and buy 2.
  • How much paint for a 10×12 room? 2 gallons for two coats on the walls of a 10×12 room with 8 ft ceilings, one door and two windows. 3 gallons with the ceiling.

Tile calculator

  • Tile calculator with waste Why a straight tile layout needs about 10% extra and herringbone needs about 20%.
  • How many boxes of tile for a shower floor? A 3×5 shower floor needs 16.5 sq ft of tile with 10% waste, which is 2 boxes of 10 sq ft.
  • How much tile for a kitchen backsplash? A 15 ft run of backsplash 18 inches tall is 22.5 sq ft, or 24.75 sq ft with 10% waste: 3 boxes of 10 sq ft tile.

Flooring calculator

  • How much flooring for a 12×12 room? 8 boxes of flooring at 20 sq ft per box for a 12×12 room laid straight, or 9 boxes laid diagonally.
  • How many boxes of flooring for 200, 500 or 1,000 sq ft? 500 sq ft of flooring laid straight takes 550 sq ft with 10% waste: 28 boxes at 20 sq ft per box. Table for 200 to 1,000 sq ft.
  • How much flooring for a 15×15 room? 13 boxes of flooring at 20 sq ft per box for a 15×15 room laid straight, or 14 boxes herringbone. Table from 10×10 to 1,000 sq ft.

Mulch calculator

  • How much mulch do I need? Cubic yards and bags from bed size. One yard covers 108 square feet at 3 inches.
  • How much mulch for 200, 300, 500 or 1,000 sq ft? Bags and cubic yards of mulch for 200 to 1,000 sq ft at 2 and 3 inches deep.
  • How many bags of mulch in a yard? One cubic yard of mulch is 27 cubic feet: 14 bags of 2 cu ft, 18 bags of 1.5 cu ft or 9 bags of 3 cu ft. It covers 108 sq ft at 3 inches.
  • How much mulch for a 10×10 bed? A 10×10 bed 3 inches deep takes 13 bags of 2 cu ft mulch, or 1 cubic yard in bulk. Table for 2, 3 and 4 inches.

Concrete calculator

  • How many concrete bags for a pad? 80 lb Quikrete bags for a 4-inch slab, using the published 0.60 cu ft yield.
  • How many bags of concrete in a yard? 45 bags of 80 lb, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb make one cubic yard, before waste.
  • How much concrete for a shed base? An 8×10 shed slab 4 inches thick takes 47 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste, about one cubic yard. Table for 8×8 to 12×16.
  • How many bags of concrete for a 10×10 slab? A 10×10 slab 4 inches thick takes 59 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste. 6 inches takes 88. Table for 60 and 80 lb bags.
  • How many bags of concrete for a 4×4 slab? A 4×4 slab 4 inches thick takes 10 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste, or 13 bags of 60 lb. 6 inches takes 14 bags of 80 lb.
