How many bags of concrete for a pad or walk?

80 lb Quikrete bags for a small 4-inch pad or walk, using the published 0.60 cu ft yield. A 4×8 pad takes 19 bags.

Quikrete’s data sheet for Concrete Mix 1101 says an 80 lb bag yields approximately 0.60 cubic feet. A 60 lb bag yields 0.45. Those are the numbers this calculator uses. Not a forum guess.

A 4×8 pad or walk section, 4 inches thick, is 10.67 cu ft. 10.67 ÷ 0.60 = 17.8 bags, which means 18 before waste. Five percent waste brings it to 18.7, so you buy 19. That is about 1,520 lb of mix, a long afternoon by hand or an easy mixer job.

Pouring a full 10×10 slab? That size has its own guide with 4, 5 and 6 inch tables.

Do not use bagged mix for a structural footing unless a local code official and the product data both say you can. This page is for pads, walks, and similar pours.
