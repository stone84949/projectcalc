How much mulch for 200, 300, 500 or 1,000 sq ft?

Bags and cubic yards of mulch for 200 to 1,000 sq ft at 2 and 3 inches deep.

At 3 inches deep, 200 sq ft takes 25 bags (2 cu ft) or 2 cubic yards. 1,000 sq ft takes 125 bags or 10 cubic yards.

Bed area2 in deep3 in deep
200 sq ft (10×20)17 bags or 2 cu yd (1.23 exact)25 bags or 2 cu yd (1.85 exact)
300 sq ft (10×30)25 bags or 2 cu yd (1.85 exact)38 bags or 3 cu yd (2.78 exact)
500 sq ft (10×50)42 bags or 4 cu yd (3.09 exact)63 bags or 5 cu yd (4.63 exact)
1,000 sq ft (20×50)84 bags or 7 cu yd (6.17 exact)125 bags or 10 cu yd (9.26 exact)

The formula is square feet × depth in inches ÷ 12 = cubic feet. Divide cubic feet by 27 for cubic yards, or by 2 for 2 cu ft bags. The calculator rounds both up, because a partial bag is still a full bag at the register.

Bag sizes vary. With 1.5 cu ft bags, 200 sq ft at 3 inches is 34 bags instead of 25. Enter the size printed on your bag.

Most beds want 2–3 inches. Keep mulch pulled back from stems and trunks.
