How many boxes of tile for a shower floor?

A 3×5 shower floor needs 16.5 sq ft of tile with 10% waste, which is 2 boxes of 10 sq ft.

A 3×5 shower floor needs 2 boxes of 10 sq ft tile. That is 15 sq ft plus 10% waste, or 16.5 sq ft.

Shower floorStraight (10%)Diagonal (20%)
3×3 ft9.9 sq ft, 1 box10.8 sq ft, 2 boxes
3×4 ft13.2 sq ft, 2 boxes14.4 sq ft, 2 boxes
3×5 ft16.5 sq ft, 2 boxes18 sq ft, 2 boxes
4×4 ft17.6 sq ft, 2 boxes19.2 sq ft, 2 boxes

Box counts assume 10 sq ft per box. Shower floors slope to the drain, so small mosaic tile is common, and mosaics are sold in sheets with their own coverage. Enter the coverage printed on your box. The calculator loads a 3×5 floor with 2-inch tile, which is 594 tiles.

A 3×3 floor laid straight fits in one 10 sq ft box with almost nothing left over. Buy a second box anyway. Small floors leave no room for a cracked tile or a bad cut.

Walls are a separate job. Run each wall as its own rectangle and add the totals.
