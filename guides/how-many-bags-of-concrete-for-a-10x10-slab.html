How many bags of concrete for a 10×10 slab?

A 10×10 slab 4 inches thick takes 59 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste. 6 inches takes 88. Table for 60 and 80 lb bags.

A 10×10 slab 4 inches thick takes 59 bags of 80 lb concrete with 5% waste. At 6 inches thick it takes 88 bags.

10 × 10 × (4 ÷ 12) = 33.33 cu ft. Five percent waste brings it to 35 cu ft. Quikrete’s data sheet for Concrete Mix 1101 lists about 0.60 cu ft per 80 lb bag, so 35 ÷ 0.60 = 58.3 bags, rounded up to 59.

That is a lot of dry mix. 59 bags of 80 lb is 4,720 lb, and 88 bags is 7,040 lb. At 1.3 to 1.9 cubic yards, compare the price of a ready-mix delivery before you mix by hand.

This is slab volume only. Gravel base, forms, rebar or mesh, and any thickened edge or footing are separate. Check local code and the product data before you pour. The calculator uses the same published yields on every slab: 0.45 cu ft per 60 lb bag and 0.60 per 80 lb bag.

FAQ

How thick should a 10×10 slab be?

Four inches is common for a patio-sized pad. Heavier loads often use more. Follow local code and the plans for your project.

Does the count include waste?

Yes, 5%. It covers spillage and an uneven base. With 0% waste a 4-inch 10×10 slab is 56 bags of 80 lb, and a 6-inch slab is 84.

Is a 10×10 slab about one cubic yard?

A 4-inch slab is 1.23 cubic yards before waste and 1.30 with 5% waste. A 6-inch slab is 1.85 before waste.
