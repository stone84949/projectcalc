How much flooring for a 12×12 room?

8 boxes of flooring at 20 sq ft per box for a 12×12 room laid straight, or 9 boxes laid diagonally.

Buy 8 boxes at 20 sq ft per box for a 12×12 room laid straight. A diagonal or herringbone layout needs 9 boxes.

12 × 12 is 144 sq ft. A straight layout adds 10% waste: 158.4 sq ft. 158.4 ÷ 20 = 7.92 boxes, so 8.

LayoutWasteSq ft to buyBoxes (20 sq ft)
Straight10%158.48
Diagonal15%165.69
Herringbone20%172.89

Boxes are often 18 to 24 sq ft, not a round 20. For the straight layout, 18 sq ft boxes means 9 boxes and 24 sq ft boxes means 7. Type the number on your box into the calculator.

Closets and doorways count. Add them as extra area in the square footage calculator first.
