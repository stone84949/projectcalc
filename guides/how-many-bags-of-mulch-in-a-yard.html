How many bags of mulch in a yard?

One cubic yard of mulch is 27 cubic feet: 14 bags of 2 cu ft, 18 bags of 1.5 cu ft or 9 bags of 3 cu ft. It covers 108 sq ft at 3 inches.

One cubic yard of mulch fills 14 bags of 2 cu ft (13.5 exact), 18 bags of 1.5 cu ft or 9 bags of 3 cu ft.

A cubic yard is 3 ft × 3 ft × 3 ft, or 27 cubic feet. Divide 27 by the bag size printed on the bag and round up. At 3 inches deep, one yard covers 108 sq ft: a 9×12 bed is exactly that, and it is the example loaded in the calculator.

FAQ

How many square feet does a yard of mulch cover?

108 sq ft at 3 inches, 162 sq ft at 2 inches, and 81 sq ft at 4 inches. Formula: 324 ÷ depth in inches.

Is a 2 cu ft bag the standard size?

It is the most common, but 1.5 and 3 cu ft bags are sold too. Read the bag and type its size into the calculator.

How deep should mulch be?

Most beds want 2–3 inches. Deeper than 4 inches can smother roots, and mulch should stay pulled back from stems and trunks.
