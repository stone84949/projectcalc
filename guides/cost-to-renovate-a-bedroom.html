2026 cost to renovate a bedroom

Planning bands for a 10×10 to 14×16 bedroom. A 12×12 bedroom plans at $2,160–$4,320 cosmetic and $5,760–$11,520 for a mid remodel.

A 12×12 bedroom plans at $2,160–$4,320 for a cosmetic refresh: paint, fixtures and light flooring. A mid remodel with new surfaces and some trades plans at $5,760–$11,520.

12 × 12 is 144 sq ft. The cosmetic band is $15–$30 per sq ft, so 144 × $15 = $2,160 and 144 × $30 = $4,320. The mid band ($40–$80) and the full gut band ($100–$200 per sq ft) are rough planning ranges, not published figures. Bedrooms and living rooms use the bands as they are, with no room factor.

Most bedroom jobs are cosmetic, and that part is mostly materials you can price yourself: run the paint and flooring calculators with the price on the shelf.

FAQ

What does a cosmetic bedroom refresh include?

Paint, light fixtures, outlets and switch plates, and light flooring work. Moving walls, windows or wiring runs is a mid or full job.

Can I lower the cost by doing it myself?

Yes, mostly labor. Use the paint and flooring calculators for the material count, then multiply by the price you see in the store.

Is a living room the same?

Yes. The calculator uses the same bands for bedrooms and living rooms. Pick “Living room” in the room type if you want the label to match.
