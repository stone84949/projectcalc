How many bags of concrete in a yard?

45 bags of 80 lb, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb make one cubic yard, before waste.

One cubic yard takes 45 bags of 80 lb concrete, 60 bags of 60 lb or 90 bags of 40 lb, before any waste.

A cubic yard is 27 cubic feet. Quikrete’s data sheet for Concrete Mix 1101 lists about 0.60 cu ft per 80 lb bag and 0.45 cu ft per 60 lb bag. 27 ÷ 0.60 = 45 and 27 ÷ 0.45 = 60. The calculator scales the 40, 50 and 90 lb sizes from the same yield per pound.

A 9×9 pad 4 inches thick is exactly 27 cu ft, one yard. That is the example loaded in the calculator, with waste set to 0. The default is 5% waste, which covers spillage and an uneven base.

45 bags of 80 lb is 3,600 lb of dry mix. At a yard or more, compare the price of a short-load ready-mix delivery before you mix by hand. Do not use bagged mix for a structural footing unless the product data and your local code official allow it.
