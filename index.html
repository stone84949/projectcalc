How much do I need, and what will it cost?

Ten free calculators for paint, tile, flooring, mulch, concrete, drywall, wallpaper, moving boxes, square footage, and room renovation cost. Every buy quantity rounds up. Coverage numbers are cited.

ProjectCalc is the first of three calculator sites on one engine. You enter room sizes. The page returns a shopping number you can take to a hardware store. It is not a contractor bid and it is not a brand funnel.

Start with paint if you are coating walls this weekend. Use square footage first if you are comparing flooring options. Open the job pack when you want a printable sheet.
