About ProjectCalc

ProjectCalc is a free suite of home DIY material calculators. It exists so you can walk into a hardware store with a number instead of a guess.

It is the first site on a shared static engine. PetCalc and SourDough Math come next, same math core, different niches. The engine has no account system and no app store.

Cost fields are whatever price you type. We do not scrape live store pricing and we do not invent a "today's Knoxville price."

ProjectCalc is a planning tool. Numbers are estimates. Confirm coverage on the product you actually buy.
