Room renovation cost calculator

Planning bands only — not a contractor bid. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 12×12 bedroom at mid finish is 144 × $40–$80, or $5,760–$11,520. A kitchen uses a 2.5× intensity on the same bands because of plumbing and cabinets.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

Is this what a Knoxville contractor will charge?

No. These are national planning bands assembled from 2025–2026 published remodel ranges. Local labor moves the number.

Why is a bathroom more than a bedroom?

Water, tile, ventilation, and code. We apply 2.2× for baths and 2.5× for kitchens.
