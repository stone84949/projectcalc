Worked example

A 12×12 bedroom at mid finish is 144 × $40–$80, or $5,760–$11,520. A kitchen uses a 2.5× intensity on the same bands because of plumbing and cabinets. A 5×8 bathroom (40 sq ft) at mid finish (keep the layout; new vanity, toilet, flooring, some tile) uses the bathroom bands of $5,000–$10,000 plus $100–$200 per sq ft, or $9,000–$18,000, close to This Old House’s $8,000–$18,000 mid-range update. Replacing everything (tub or shower, tile, toilet, vanity, floor) is the full tier: a 5×8 plans at $16,000–$30,000. This Old House’s $15,900–$20,100 for a 5×8 (June 2026) and the 2025 Cost vs. Value midrange bath ($26,138 for a 5×7 with every fixture replaced) are that full-replacement scope.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

Is this what a Knoxville contractor will charge?

No. These are national planning bands assembled from 2025–2026 published remodel ranges. Local labor moves the number.

Why is a bathroom more than a bedroom?

Water, tile, waterproofing, ventilation, plumbing, and code. Kitchens use 2.5× the general bands. Bathrooms use their own bands: a fixed base for fixtures, plumbing and waterproofing plus a per-sq-ft part (cosmetic $2,000–$4,000 + $25–$100, mid $5,000–$10,000 + $100–$200, full $6,000–$14,000 + $250–$400 per sq ft). They were set in October 2026 from This Old House (June 2026, Homewyse data), the 2025 Cost vs. Value report, Fixr (January 2026), Angi (July 2026) and HomeAdvisor (June 2026). The split between base and per-sq-ft is our judgment between those sources.

Does it work for a big primary bathroom?

Yes, roughly. Because part of the cost is a fixed base, cost per sq ft falls as the room grows: a 10×10 primary bath plans at $15,000–$30,000 for a mid-range update, in line with HomeAdvisor’s $7,000–$30,000 primary-bath range. Layout changes, moved plumbing and premium finishes push past the full tier, so get bids.
