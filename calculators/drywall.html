Drywall calculator

4×8 sheets, waste, screws, and tape for a room. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 12×12×8 room with one door and the ceiling is 507 sq ft net. Fifteen percent waste is 583 sq ft. At 32 sq ft per 4×8 sheet you buy 19 sheets.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

Should I subtract doors and windows?

We subtract openings and add 15% waste. CertainTeed’s public calculator does not subtract openings and does not add waste. Net results are often close.

4×8 or 4×12?

Taller sheets mean fewer butt joints. Enter 4 and 12 in a future pass — this MVP defaults to 4×8, the sheet most stores stack.
