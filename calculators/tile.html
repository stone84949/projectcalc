Tile calculator

Floor area plus layout waste, then whole boxes. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 10×10 floor is 100 sq ft. Straight layout waste is 10%, so you need 110 sq ft. With 12-inch tile and 10 sq ft boxes that is 11 boxes.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

How much waste should I add?

Straight or grid: 10%. Offset: 15%. Diagonal or herringbone: 20%. Large-format tile can need more.

Can I reuse the offcuts?

On a straight lay, often. On herringbone, almost never — every wall gets a diagonal cut.
