Square footage calculator

Add rooms and get a floor-area total you can reuse. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 12×12 bedroom (144) plus a 10×14 office (140) is 284 sq ft. Feed that number into flooring or tile next.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

Do I include closets?

Yes if you are flooring or painting them. Add each closet as its own room.

What about irregular rooms?

Split the room into rectangles and add them. Do not average odd walls.
