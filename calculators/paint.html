Paint calculator

How much paint for a room — gallons, coats, and a shopping number. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 12×12 room with 8 ft ceilings, one door, and two windows has 384 sq ft of wall. Subtract 51 sq ft of openings. Two coats is 666 sq ft. At 375 sq ft per gallon that is 1.776 gallons, so you buy 2 gallons.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

How much paint for a 12×12 room?

Usually two gallons for walls only at two coats, using Sherwin-Williams’ 350–400 sq ft per gallon range. Ceilings add the floor area.

Do you subtract doors and windows?

Yes. A door is 21 sq ft (3×7) and a window is 15 sq ft (3×5). If your openings are bigger, raise the counts or treat extra area as another window.

Why not just use floor square footage?

Floor area is not wall area. A 12×12 room is 144 sq ft on the floor and 384 sq ft on the walls at 8 ft.
