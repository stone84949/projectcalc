Mulch calculator

Cubic yards and bags from bed size and depth. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 10×20 bed at 3 inches is 50 cubic feet, which is 1.852 cubic yards. You order 2 cubic yards bulk, or 25 two-cubic-foot bags.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

How much mulch for 3 inches?

One cubic yard covers 108 sq ft at 3 inches. Formula: square feet × depth in inches ÷ 324 = cubic yards.

Bags or bulk?

Under about 2 yards, bags are easier. Above that, bulk usually wins on price and plastic waste.
