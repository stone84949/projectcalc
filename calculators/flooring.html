Flooring calculator

Laminate, LVP, or hardwood boxes with waste built in. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 12×15 room is 180 sq ft. Ten percent waste is 198 sq ft. At 20 sq ft per box you buy 10 boxes.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

Does this work for LVP and hardwood?

The area math is the same. Read the box for actual coverage — it is often 18 to 24 sq ft, not a round 20.

Why do stores tell me to buy extra?

Dye lots change. A leftover box from the same lot is cheaper than a mismatch in six months.
