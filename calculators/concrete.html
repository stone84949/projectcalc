Concrete bag calculator

Bags for a slab using Quikrete published yields. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

A 10×10 pad, 4 inches thick, is 33.333 cu ft. An 80 lb bag yields 0.60 cu ft, so you need 56 bags before waste and 59 bags with 5% waste.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

How many 80 lb bags in a cubic yard?

A cubic yard is 27 cu ft. 27 ÷ 0.60 = 45 bags, before waste.

Should I mix this many bags by hand?

A 10×10×4 pad is a mixer job. Budget water, a helper, and a continuous pour so you do not get a cold joint.
