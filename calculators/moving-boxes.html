Moving boxes calculator

Box counts by room mix and how packed the house is. Estimate only — buy what the can, bag, or box in your hand actually covers.

This is a planning estimate, not a bid and not product advice. Confirm coverage on the technical data sheet.

Worked example

One typical kitchen plus one other bedroom is 5 small, 10 medium, 6 large, 2 XL, 3 wardrobe, and 5 dish packs — 31 boxes.

How the math works

Inputs are feet and inches. The engine keeps everything in square feet or cubic feet, applies a published coverage or waste factor, then rounds the buy quantity up. Partial bags are still a full bag at the register.

FAQ

Why is this a range in other articles?

Households vary. We use published midpoints and let you pick sparse, typical, or packed.

Do I need dish packs?

If the kitchen is in the count, yes. Regular boxes crush stemware.
